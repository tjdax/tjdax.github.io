Stick 'n Snap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how the Stick 'n Snap Android app (“the app”) handles information. The app is made for children and families. A parent or guardian should help a child use it.

Who we are

Stick 'n Snap is a sticker camera app. Contact: tjdaxmail@gmail.com

Photos and stickers

  • Photos taken or picked in the app stay on the device.
  • We do not upload children’s photos to our servers.
  • Saved pictures are written to the device gallery (Pictures/StickNSnap) so the family can keep them.
  • Every sticker pack is free. The language preference is stored only on the device.

Ads

There are no ads on the sticker screen. The only ad is an optional “Support us” video in Settings. It plays only when someone taps that button, and it does not unlock anything.

The video is served by Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and limited to content rated G, so Google does not show personalized ads. The app does not use the Android advertising ID.

To serve ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK sends Google some information from the device: the IP address (which Google can use to estimate a rough location), device and app information, how the ad was viewed, and diagnostic and crash data from the SDK. Google handles this as described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What we do not collect

  • No account or login
  • No name, email, or phone number
  • No photo upload to Stick 'n Snap servers
  • No analytics or crash reporting tools of our own
  • No in-app purchases

Children

The app is intended for children about 4 to 10 years old with a parent nearby. We do not collect personal information from children on our own servers. The only information that leaves the device is what the AdMob SDK sends to Google, described above. If you have a question about a child’s data, contact us at tjdaxmail@gmail.com.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take a photo for the canvas
  • Internet: to load the optional Support us video
  • Photos / storage: to pick a picture and save the result

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
