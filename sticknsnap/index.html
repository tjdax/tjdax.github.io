Stick 'n Snap Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy explains how the Stick 'n Snap Android app (“the app”) handles information. The app is made for children and families. A parent or guardian should help a child use it.

Who we are

Stick 'n Snap is a sticker camera app. Contact: tjdaxmail@gmail.com

Photos and stickers

  • Photos taken or picked in the app stay on the device.
  • We do not upload children’s photos to our servers.
  • Saved pictures are written to the device gallery (Pictures/StickNSnap) so the family can keep them.
  • Unlock choices and language preference are stored only on the device.

Ads

The app uses Google AdMob rewarded video ads to unlock extra sticker packs. Settings also has an optional “Support us” button. Watching that ad does not unlock new packs; it is only a way to support the app after packs are already unlocked.

Google may use an advertising identifier and standard ad signals. Google’s ads are covered by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Ads policy.

Parents should use a child-appropriate ads setup in AdMob / Play Families if they publish a family listing.

Data we do not collect

  • No account or login
  • No name, email, or phone number inside the app
  • No location
  • No photo upload to Stick 'n Snap servers

Children

The app is intended for children about 4 to 10 years old with a parent nearby. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children on our own servers. Ads are provided by Google. If you believe a child has provided personal data through ads, contact us and Google as needed.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take a photo for the canvas
  • Internet: to load rewarded ads
  • Photos / storage: to pick a picture and save the result

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
