HueSkin

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Android package: com.hueskin.app

Summary

  • HueSkin analyzes skin type and personal color on your device. Face photos are stored on this phone only and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, and no HueSkin backend for photos or analysis.
  • Internet is used for ads, optional crash/analytics, and in-app purchase. Analysis still works offline.
  • This app is not a medical device and does not provide a medical diagnosis.

1. Who we are

This policy describes how the HueSkin Android app (“HueSkin”, “we”) handles information. Contact: tjdaxmail@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

HueSkin does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or other identity details for an account (there is no sign-up)
  • Face photos or analysis images to our own servers
  • Precise GPS location (the app does not request location permission)
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS, or files outside the photos you choose in the app

3. Camera, photos, and on-device analysis

HueSkin uses the camera (or a photo you pick from the gallery) so the app can estimate skin-related scores and personal-color palettes on the device. Processing uses on-device face landmarks. Photos, heatmaps, draping previews, and history records are saved in the app’s private storage on your phone.

We do not operate a server that receives those images. If you share a report, sharing uses the Android share sheet that you control (for example another app you choose).

You can delete history records in the app. Uninstalling HueSkin removes local photos and analysis data stored by the app.

4. Settings stored on the device

Language, optional local skincare reminders, and whether ads were removed are stored on the device (app preferences). Reminder notifications are scheduled locally. Notification permission is optional.

5. Advertising

HueSkin shows Google AdMob banner ads on some screens (for example Home and reports). An optional rewarded ad may appear only if you tap “Watch an ad to support” in Settings. Ads are not required to unlock analysis.

When ads are requested, Google AdMob and related Google advertising services may process:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device and app information
  • IP address and coarse location derived from IP
  • Ad interaction data

We do not receive your face photos through AdMob. Google handles ad data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings. Purchasing “Remove ads” in Settings stops HueSkin from showing its banners and the optional support ad.

6. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

HueSkin uses Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) to understand app stability and aggregated usage. Firebase may process app events, crash logs, device and app version information, and a Firebase installation identifier. This data is used to fix bugs and improve the app. It is not used to identify you by name.

Firebase policies: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

7. In-app purchases

Optional “Remove ads” is processed by Google Play Billing. Purchase records are handled by Google under the Google Play terms. HueSkin stores a local flag on the device so ads can stay removed after purchase.

8. Internet access

The INTERNET permission is used for ads, Firebase, and Play Billing. Skin analysis and personal-color analysis do not require a network connection.

9. Children’s privacy

HueSkin is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Medical disclaimer

HueSkin reports are for general reference and beauty information only. They are not a medical diagnosis, treatment, or substitute for advice from a qualified professional.

11. Data retention and deletion

On-device photos and history remain until you delete them in the app or uninstall HueSkin. Advertising, analytics, and crash data retained by Google are subject to Google’s retention practices. To request help about this policy, email tjdaxmail@gmail.com.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised. Continued use of HueSkin after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

13. Contact

Email: tjdaxmail@gmail.com
