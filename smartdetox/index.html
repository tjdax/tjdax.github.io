SmartDetox Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

SmartDetox is an Android app that blocks apps you choose, or the rest of the phone, during a rest, a schedule, or after a daily limit you set. The developer is reachable at tjdaxmail@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

Rules, the rest timer, per-app time totals, and the number of blocks are stored only on the device. SmartDetox has no account and no server of its own. Uninstalling the app deletes that data.

Accessibility

If you turn the service on, Android sends SmartDetox the package name of the app in the foreground. The app uses that name to apply your rules, show a block screen, leave a blocked app, and optionally remove color. It does not read screen text, messages, passwords, photos, or contacts.

Calls, emergency calling, Settings, and SmartDetox stay available so you can turn the protection off.

Ads and purchases

A banner may be shown at the bottom of the app. In Settings you can watch a rewarded ad to support the app with a coffee. Google AdMob can use the advertising ID and related device data under Google's policy. A one-time purchase through Google Play removes ads and stops further ad requests from SmartDetox. There is no donation or email-support button.

Permissions

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts. Notifications are used to show that a rest or schedule is active. Grayscale, if you enable it, uses Android color correction and may require a secure setting that only you can grant.
