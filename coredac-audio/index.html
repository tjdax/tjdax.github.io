Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Android package: com.coredac.audio

CoreDAC Audio plays music stored on the device and on network storage the user enters. It does not ask you to create an account.

Data we do not collect

The developer does not receive an account, contacts, location, advertising ID, or listening history. This app has no analytics SDK and no ads.

Data kept on the device

  • Tracks you choose with the music permission
  • SMB, WebDAV, and DLNA addresses and passwords you type
  • Equalizer, buffer, and USB output settings
  • Whether CoreDAC Pro is owned on this Google account

This stays in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the app. A network password is sent only to the NAS you specify, and only to connect.

Purchases

CoreDAC Pro is a one-time purchase sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment under the Google Play terms, and the developer does not receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play on the device whether Pro is owned and stores only that yes or no result on the device.

Permissions

The app uses music access, notifications, internet, Google Play billing, USB host, and Wi-Fi multicast for DLNA discovery. USB permission is requested only when sending audio directly to a DAC.

Children

CoreDAC Audio is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
