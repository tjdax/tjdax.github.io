UnitSlide Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

UnitSlide converts units on your phone. It has no account and no app server.

What stays on the device

The app stores three settings on the phone only: the language you pick, whether banner ads were removed, and the last conversion you opened. Unit math does not leave the device.

Advertising

Until you buy ad removal, UnitSlide shows a Google AdMob banner. A button in Settings can play an optional rewarded video. AdMob may use the Android advertising ID to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Buying “Remove ads” turns off the banner on that phone. The optional support video is still available if you choose to play it.

Purchases

Ad removal is a one-time Google Play in-app product. Google Play processes the payment. UnitSlide only stores a local flag that the banner should stay off, and it checks Play to restore that purchase.

What we do not collect

UnitSlide does not ask for your name, email, location, photos, contacts, or microphone. It does not use Firebase or any other analytics SDK.

Children

UnitSlide is not directed at children under 13.
