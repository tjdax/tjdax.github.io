EntityRadar 2 Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

EntityRadar 2 Pro is a paid entertainment app. It has no account, no ads, and no app server.

While the app is on screen it may read the phone sensors, microphone, Wi-Fi strength, cellular signal strength, and location. Those readings are used to draw the radar, the sound lab, and the map pins. They stay on the phone. When the camera is on, frames are checked on the phone for a human pose to draw a stick figure; frames are not uploaded.

Weather lookups send approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo. Encounter logs, photos, and videos are not uploaded.

Photos save to Pictures/EntityRadar2Pro and videos to Movies/EntityRadar2Pro. You can delete map marks inside the app.

The app does not ask for your name, email, or contacts. It is not directed at children under 13.
